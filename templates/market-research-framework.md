# Market Research Framework

## Purpose

This template provides a structured approach for researching the market before committing to an AI business idea. It covers segment sizing, competitive analysis, pricing signals, search demand, and community evidence.

Market research does not replace customer discovery. It is the desk research you do before and alongside direct customer conversations.

### 8. Red Flags

**Signals that suggest caution or reconsideration:**

- [ ] No competitors exist and no clear demand signal
- [ ] Competitors exist but are not charging (free market)
- [ ] Market is dominated by one large player with high switching costs
- [ ] Regulatory or compliance barriers are high
- [ ] Buyers are in a cost-cutting phase
- [ ] Search demand is declining
- [ ] Community discussions show failed previous solutions
