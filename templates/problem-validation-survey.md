# Problem Validation Survey

## Purpose

This template helps you structure your problem validation research before committing to building. Complete this document before starting customer discovery interviews and update it after each interview round.

A completed problem validation survey gives you a structured view of the evidence for and against the problem hypothesis. It reduces the risk of building something no one needs.
