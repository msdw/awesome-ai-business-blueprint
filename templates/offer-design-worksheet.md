# Offer Design Worksheet

## Purpose

This worksheet helps you design a specific, bounded, and communicable offer before going to market. A well-designed offer reduces buyer hesitation, prevents scope creep, and makes it easier to price and deliver consistently.

Complete this worksheet after validating the problem and selecting a business model. Review it with a potential customer before finalizing.
