# Weekly Metrics Tracker

## Purpose

This template provides a simple weekly metrics tracking structure for AI products and services in their early stage. Consistent weekly tracking allows you to identify trends, catch problems early, and make decisions based on evidence rather than gut feel.

Track no more than 5–7 metrics. If you track everything, you prioritize nothing.

### Weekly Review Questions

Answer these questions at the end of each week:

1. **What is the most important thing the data tells us this week?**

2. **What is the biggest problem to solve before next week?**

3. **What did we ship or change this week?**

4. **What feedback did we receive from customers?**

5. **What is the top priority for next week?**
