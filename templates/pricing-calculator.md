# Pricing Calculator Template

## Purpose

This template helps you calculate a sustainable price for your AI product or service. It starts from your delivery costs and buyer value, not from competitor prices or gut feel.

Pricing is a hypothesis until tested. Use this template to build the hypothesis, then test it in real sales conversations.
