# AI Business Blueprint — Overview

The AI Business Blueprint is a 15-step, structured methodology for turning an AI use case into a validated, operational business. It is designed for founders, consultants, product managers, and builders who want to move from curiosity to execution without relying on hype or guesswork.

## The Framework at a Glance

```
Problem → Customer → Use Case → Business Model → Offer → MVP → Technical Stack → Pricing → Go-to-Market → Distribution → Delivery → Risk → Growth → Scaling
```

## What Each Step Covers

**Step 1 — Problem Identification**: Define a specific, observable, and painful problem. Evaluate frequency, severity, and economic cost before touching any AI tool.

**Step 2 — Market Validation**: Verify that a sufficient number of potential paying buyers exist. Analyze market size with realism, study competitors, and gather market signals from job boards, forums, and search volume.

**Step 3 — Customer Discovery**: Conduct direct interviews with target customers. Understand their current workflows, the cost of the problem, their failed attempts, and their willingness to pay.

**Step 4 — AI Use Case Design**: Define precisely what the AI does in the workflow — input, task, output. Evaluate why AI improves on manual or rule-based alternatives, and identify failure modes.

**Step 5 — Business Model Selection**: Choose a monetization model that fits the value delivered and the buyer context. Draft a pricing hypothesis and a unit economics sketch.

**Step 6 — Offer Design**: Translate the use case and business model into a clear, bounded, sellable offer with defined scope, deliverables, timeline, and price.

**Step 7 — MVP Planning**: Define the smallest testable version of the product or service that delivers core value to one real customer. Determine what can be done manually before automating.

**Step 8 — Technical Stack**: Select the simplest technical architecture that ships the MVP within the target timeframe. Choose LLM APIs, infrastructure, and integrations with cost estimates.

**Step 9 — Pricing Strategy**: Set a pricing strategy grounded in buyer ROI, comparable products, and your own cost structure. Draft a pricing page and model revenue at different scale assumptions.

**Step 10 — Go-to-Market**: Plan how to reach the first 10 paying customers. Define the channel, message, and 30-day outreach sequence.

**Step 11 — Distribution Channels**: Identify and prioritize the channels that will drive sustainable customer acquisition beyond the initial launch.

**Step 12 — Delivery and Operations**: Define the delivery process step by step, establish quality gates, and calculate the cost of delivery per customer.

**Step 13 — Risk and Compliance**: Build a risk register covering technical, market, legal, privacy, and operational risks. Add compliance notes for regulated domains.

**Step 14 — Growth and Iteration**: Establish the feedback loop and tracking system that allows the product to improve based on real usage data.

**Step 15 — Scaling**: Identify the right moment to scale, validate product-market fit, and plan the next 12 months of growth with realistic unit economics.

## How to Use This Blueprint

Start at Step 1 even if you already have an AI idea. The temptation to skip early steps is the most common source of wasted effort. Each step produces a concrete deliverable that feeds the next.

Use the templates in `templates/` at each step. Use the canvases in `canvases/` to structure your thinking visually. Reference the examples in `data/examples.yaml` to see how the blueprint applies to concrete cases.

Every business idea is a hypothesis until validated by real customers.
