## What This Is

A complete, practical business blueprint for AI builders. It includes:

- **15 structured chapters** — covering every stage from problem validation to scaling
- **7 canvases** — AI Business Canvas, Offer Canvas, MVP Canvas, and more
- **Templates** — customer interview scripts, pricing sheets, delivery checklists
- **Real examples** — documented AI businesses with honest outcome data

## What This Is Not

- Not financial advice or investment recommendations
- Not a promise of revenue or success
- Not a "startup success formula"
- Not a substitute for customer discovery and market research
