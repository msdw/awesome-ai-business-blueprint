# Contributing to Awesome AI Business Blueprint

All submissions are reviewed by maintainers before merging.

## Ways to Contribute

### Suggest an example

Open an issue using the [Suggest Example template](https://github.com/msdw/awesome-ai-business-blueprint/issues/new?template=suggest-example.yml).

Good examples are specific, include honest outcome data, and map to a specific blueprint stage.

### Suggest a resource

Open a [Suggest Resource](https://github.com/msdw/awesome-ai-business-blueprint/issues/new?template=suggest-resource.yml) issue.

### Report a problem

Open a [Problem Report](https://github.com/msdw/awesome-ai-business-blueprint/issues/new?template=report-problem.yml) for broken links, outdated content, or quality issues.

## Quality Bar

### What gets accepted

- Examples with honest data (revenue, users, timeframes — including failures)
- Resources directly relevant to business building, not just "popular in AI"
- Templates that are immediately usable

### What gets rejected

- "Guaranteed revenue" or "passive income" claims
- Examples without verifiable outcome data
- Generic AI tool lists with no editorial value
