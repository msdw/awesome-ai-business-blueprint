# AI Go-to-Market Canvas

## Purpose

The AI Go-to-Market Canvas structures the plan for reaching and converting the first paying customers. It replaces vague intentions ("post on LinkedIn," "launch on Product Hunt") with a specific, executable sequence of actions tied to a real buyer persona and a defined conversion path.

Use this canvas during Step 10 (Go-to-Market) after the offer is defined and the MVP is ready or nearly ready. Update it as you learn from actual sales conversations.

A GTM canvas is not a marketing plan. It is a focused, time-bound effort to acquire the first 10 customers.

## How to Use

1. Fill in every section with specific information, not generalities.
2. The "First 20 Prospects" section must contain named or clearly described real organizations or people.
3. Set the conversion milestone before starting outreach.
4. Review weekly and update based on actual responses.

---

## Canvas Template

| Section | Your Plan |
|---|---|
| **Niche** | The specific, narrow market segment you are targeting first. (Not "SMBs" — "operations managers at logistics companies with 50–200 employees") |
| **Buyer Persona** | The specific person who signs the contract: role, typical organization, budget, decision-making authority. |
| **Pain Message** | The one-sentence description of the problem, in buyer language. This is your outreach hook. |
| **Proof Point** | The evidence that reduces buyer risk: a case study, a demo, a reference customer, measurable results. |
| **Channel** | The 1–2 channels you will use to reach the buyer: LinkedIn outreach, cold email, community, referral, content, or event. |
| **First 20 Prospects** | A list of 20 specific organizations or individuals you will contact in the first two weeks. |
| **Demo Assets** | What you will show or send to interested prospects: a demo video, a one-pager, a live walkthrough, a pilot offer. |
| **Objections** | The top 3 objections you expect. How you will respond to each. |
| **Call to Action** | What you are asking the prospect to do: a 20-minute call, a pilot agreement, a demo. |
| **Conversion Metric** | The observable outcome that defines success for the first 30 days: number of calls, number of pilots, number of paying customers. |

---

## GTM Readiness Check

- [ ] The niche is specific enough to name 20 real prospects
- [ ] The pain message uses buyer language, not technical language
- [ ] The call to action is low-commitment and specific
- [ ] At least one proof point exists or is being prepared
- [ ] The conversion metric is defined before outreach starts
