# AI Business Canvas

## Purpose

The AI Business Canvas is a one-page strategic tool for structuring an AI-powered business hypothesis. It captures the most important dimensions of the business on a single page, making it easy to communicate, test, and iterate.

Use this canvas at the beginning of the blueprint process (Steps 1–6) and update it as your assumptions are validated or revised. Each cell is a hypothesis, not a fact.

The canvas does not replace customer discovery or market validation. It structures what you know and what you still need to learn.

## How to Use

1. Fill in each section with your current best hypothesis.
2. Mark sections where you have validated evidence vs. assumptions.
3. Review with potential customers or advisors.
4. Update after each customer discovery conversation or experiment.

---

## Canvas Template

| Section | Your Hypothesis |
|---|---|
| **Customer Segment** | Who specifically has this problem? (role, industry, size) |
| **Pain Point** | What specific, painful problem do they face? |
| **Current Workaround** | How do they solve it today? What is frustrating about that? |
| **AI-Enabled Value Proposition** | What does the AI solution make possible that was not possible before? |
| **Offer** | What exactly are you selling? (product, service, package, license) |
| **Business Model** | How do you charge? (subscription, project fee, usage, license) |
| **MVP** | What is the smallest version that delivers the core value? |
| **Distribution Channel** | How will you reach the first 10 paying customers? |
| **Pricing Hypothesis** | What is the price range? What is the ROI for the buyer? |
| **Data Requirements** | What data does the AI need? Where does it come from? |
| **Risks** | Top 3 risks: technical, market, legal, or operational |
| **Validation Evidence** | What evidence have you collected so far? What is still assumed? |
| **Next Experiment** | What is the one experiment that would most reduce your biggest assumption? |

---

## Validation Status Key

- `[A]` — Assumption, not yet validated
- `[E]` — Supported by evidence (interview, test, data)
- `[V]` — Validated through direct customer feedback or sale
