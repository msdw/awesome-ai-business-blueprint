# AI Offer Canvas

## Purpose

The AI Offer Canvas helps you design a specific, bounded, and communicable offer before going to market. A clear offer reduces buyer hesitation, shortens sales cycles, and prevents scope creep.

Use this canvas during Step 6 (Offer Design) after you have identified the customer, the problem, and the business model. Update it as you learn from sales conversations.

A strong offer answers every question a skeptical buyer would ask before making a decision. If any cell is vague, the offer is not ready.

## How to Use

1. Fill in every section before your first sales conversation.
2. Review with a potential customer to identify which sections create confusion or objections.
3. Refine based on feedback.
4. Use the final version as the foundation for your proposal template.

---

## Canvas Template

| Section | Your Definition |
|---|---|
| **Target Buyer** | Who signs the contract? (role, organization type, budget range) |
| **User** | Who uses the solution day-to-day? (may differ from buyer) |
| **Pain** | What specific problem does the buyer want solved? In their words. |
| **Outcome Promised** | What will be measurably better after the engagement? |
| **Deliverables** | What concrete outputs will the buyer receive? (system, report, dashboard, trained model) |
| **Scope** | What is explicitly included in this offer? |
| **Exclusions** | What is explicitly NOT included? |
| **Timeline** | How long does delivery take from signed agreement to completion? |
| **Pricing** | What does this cost? What payment structure applies? |
| **Proof Required** | What evidence reduces buyer risk enough to sign? (case study, demo, pilot, reference) |
| **Success Criteria** | How will both parties agree that the engagement succeeded? |
| **Support Model** | What support is included after delivery? Duration, response time, scope. |

---

## Offer Strength Check

Before presenting this offer, verify:

- [ ] Every section is specific, not vague
- [ ] The exclusions list is explicitly written
- [ ] The success criteria are measurable
- [ ] The pricing reflects your actual delivery cost plus margin
- [ ] The offer avoids promising specific AI accuracy or performance levels
