# AI Operating Model Canvas

## Purpose

The AI Operating Model Canvas structures the operational systems required to deliver your AI product or service reliably and repeatedly. It covers the full lifecycle from customer onboarding to renewal, including the human and automated components of each step.

Use this canvas during Step 12 (Delivery and Operations) after the first customer is onboarded. Update it as the delivery process is refined and as volume grows.

An operating model is not just about delivery — it is about making delivery repeatable, delegatable, and improvable over time. A founder-dependent operating model cannot scale.

## How to Use

1. Fill in every section based on how you currently operate or intend to operate.
2. Identify the steps that are currently undocumented, manual, or founder-dependent.
3. Prioritize documentation and standardization for the highest-frequency steps first.
4. Review quarterly and update as the product and team evolve.

## Canvas Template

| Section | Your Process |
|---|---|
| **Onboarding** | How does a new customer get started? What do they provide? What do you configure? How long does it take? |
| **Data Intake** | How does customer data enter the system? What format? What validation is performed? Who has access? |
| **Configuration** | What must be configured for each customer? What is standardized vs. customized? |
| **Human Review** | Which AI outputs require human review before reaching the customer? Who reviews? What is the review criteria? |
| **Quality Monitoring** | How is output quality tracked over time? What triggers a quality alert? Who is responsible? |
| **Support** | What support channels are available? What response time is promised? What is in and out of scope? |
| **Maintenance** | How is the AI system maintained? What triggers a model update, prompt update, or configuration change? |
| **Reporting** | What do customers receive as reporting? How often? In what format? |
| **Security** | How is customer data protected? Who has access? How are access controls reviewed? |
| **Change Management** | How are customers notified of product changes? What change requires their approval? |
| **Renewal** | What triggers a renewal conversation? Who initiates it? What is the success evidence presented? |

## Operating Model Maturity Check

| Level | Description |
|---|---|
| Undocumented | Process exists only in the founder's head |
| Documented | Process is written down but not templated |
| Templated | Process uses repeatable templates and checklists |
| Delegatable | Process can be executed by someone other than the founder |
| Automated | Process runs without manual intervention |
